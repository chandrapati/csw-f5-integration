What the F5 connector adds

The BIG-IP pushes IPFIX for both halves. The F5 connector correlates them and annotates each one, so Secure Workload can follow the real client to the real backend. The two records stay two records.

A policy written on 198.51.100.200 allows every client that shares that SNAT pool. The External Orchestrator is a separate path: it names this VIP service_name = payments-vip so policy can name the service.
