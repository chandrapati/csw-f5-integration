Nothing flows until the publisher is attached.

The pool, the destination, and the publisher can all exist while the virtual server stays silent. Attachment is the step that starts IPFIX.

1

Pool

Where the records go — the F5 connector on the Ingest appliance.

2

IPFIX log destination

What format. Protocol IPFIX, transport UDP.

3

Log publisher

The named handle the logging profile refers to.

4

Attached to the virtual server

Normally through an AFM network-firewall logging profile. This is what starts the export.

AFM is the primary documented path. AVR and an iRule are named alternatives to confirm on that TMOS. Exact AFM menu labels vary by release — verify them on the box. The F5 connector listens on UDP 4739, not the NetFlow connector on 4729.
